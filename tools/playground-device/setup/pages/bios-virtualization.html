One BIOS setting needs turning on

This PC has hardware virtualization switched off in its BIOS/UEFI settings. The Playground needs it to run its private workspace.

What to do

  1. Restart the PC and press the setup key your PC shows at startup (often F2, F10, F12 or Delete).
  2. Find a setting named like Virtualization, Intel VT-x, AMD SVM or Virtualization Technology and set it to Enabled.
  3. Save and exit (usually F10). Windows starts as usual.
  4. Run setup again — it picks up where it left off.

Your PC's manual or maker support site shows the exact menu for your model. Nothing else about your PC changes.
