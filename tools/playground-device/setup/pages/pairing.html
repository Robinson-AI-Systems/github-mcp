One check, then approve

Your computer just introduced itself. On the My connection page you will see a signing-key fingerprint for this computer.

Compare the two fingerprints. They must match exactly — this is what proves the approval goes to YOUR computer and not someone else's.

Then press Approve on My connection. Setup finishes by itself: a readiness check runs, and the computer shows as Ready.

If the fingerprints do not match, do not approve — close setup and start again.
